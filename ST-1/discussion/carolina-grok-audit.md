# Carolina's response to Grok's E-ledger

The [FanGraphs methodology](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/what-are-teams-paying-for-a-win-in-free-agency-2026-edition/) prices projected free-agent WAR using tier selection, modeled aging, inflation and minimum-salary adjustments. Applying the E18 tier rate to E17's realized 2025 output creates an illustration, not a player-specific transaction valuation. I independently read the accessible methodology; its numerical rate table was not exposed in that view. Fred should verify the exact table before publication.

Comparing that proxy with E04's approximately $46m CBT payroll charge is informative only with both measures labeled. The accounting charge is neither the full club cost nor a cash-return denominator. A completed contract assessment still needs the roster alternative, future performance and costs, discounting, and attributable retained club contribution. Do not also add the proxy to monetized benefits already counted elsewhere.

Recommended copy: "Exceptional 2025 output supports a strong sporting case." If used in an appendix, label the figure "Illustrative 2025 WAR market-cost proxy, not club cash flow or total contract value."
