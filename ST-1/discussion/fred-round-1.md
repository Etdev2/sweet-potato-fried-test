# Fred, Round 1

My remit is finance audit and cash-flow modeling. My macro task is to test whether Ohtani creates sufficient incremental club cash flow and whether a U.S. advertiser can justify its own spend.

Three atomic tasks:
1. Reconcile contractual obligations, payment timing, economic present value and competitive balance tax accounting.
2. Model incremental club contribution, attribution, downside and break-even.
3. Separate athlete endorsement receipts from advertiser campaign economics and audit every financial slide.

Alongside Fred and Carolina, I recommend Nico as U.S. market and endorsement researcher, a baseball analyst for performance and injury scenarios, a creative director for advertiser propositions, and an evidence editor/deck producer for citations and decks.

MLB reports $700m nominal: $2m annually during 2024–33, then $68m annually during 2034–43; approximately $46m annual CBT charge. [MLB explanation](https://www.mlb.com/news/shohei-ohtani-dodgers-deal-deferrals-explained).

Skeptical argument: popularity does not establish incremental profit. Club sponsorship receipts, athlete endorsement receipts and advertisers' sales belong to different entities. Deferral changes payment timing; it does not eliminate the obligation. Crediting all Japanese sponsorship growth or merchandise sales to Ohtani would overstate return without attribution, margins and revenue-sharing evidence.

Proposed definitions:
- Contract nominal value is the undiscounted sum of guaranteed contractual compensation.
- Present value is the discounted cash-payment schedule at an explicit valuation date and discount rate. MLB's prescribed CBT calculation is a separate accounting measure.
- Endorsement income is athlete compensation for commercial rights and services over a period, with gross/net and cash/accrual basis specified. U.S. income requires a documented territorial allocation.
- Club value here is the incremental present value of club cash flows attributable to Ohtani versus a stated alternative, after costs, tax and sharing. Franchise enterprise value is separate.

Proposed assumptions: show original-decision and current-outlook views separately; use pretax nominal dollars, annual payment timing, and 3%/5%/7% discount sensitivities. Treat endorsement fees, U.S. allocation, funding obligations and attribution as unresolved inputs.
