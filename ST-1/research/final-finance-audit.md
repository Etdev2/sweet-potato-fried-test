## E18 benchmark

The arithmetic is correct: 9.4 times $13.91m equals $130.754m, rounded to $131m. The numerical difference against $46m is $84.754m, and the ratio is 2.842. Neither is club profit or return.

[MLB's 2025 MVP report](https://www.mlb.com/news/2025-mlb-mvp-award-winners) verifies Ohtani's combined 9.4 FanGraphs WAR.

[Ben Clemens, FanGraphs, February 24, 2026](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/what-are-teams-paying-for-a-win-in-free-agency-2026-edition/) verifies E18's $14.13m, $13.91m and $12.84m rates for 2024, 2025 and 2026. These are cohorts defined by first-year Steamer projections, with multi-year aging, inflation, minimum-salary adjustments and normalization to 2025 dollars. They are not marginal prices assigned separately to the first, second and later wins. Applying the cohort rate to realized WAR is a retrospective proxy. The article body does not document CBT AAV treatment of deferred contracts; remove that E18 assertion pending evidence. A full contract test must address future availability and costs. Do not combine this proxy with monetized win revenue as two benefits.
