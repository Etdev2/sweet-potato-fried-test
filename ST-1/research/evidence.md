## Answer key: does endorsement income establish contract value?

**No — E06–E09 are player income, not club value.** E06/E07/E08 are worldwide estimates paid to Ohtani; they do not offset the Dodgers' obligation (E01–E05). Club-side commercial evidence is E10–E15, which is real but mostly undisclosed or not isolable to Ohtani. The cleanest contract-value test is on-field: E16–E17 × E18 against E03/E04. Illustration only (Fred to verify): 9.4 WAR × $13.91M ≈ $131M of 2025 market-rate value vs. ≈ $46M CBT charge.
