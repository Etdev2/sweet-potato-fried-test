## Answer key: does endorsement income establish contract value?

**No — E06–E09 are player income, not club value.** E06/E07/E08 are worldwide estimates paid to Ohtani; they do not offset the Dodgers' obligation (E01–E05). Club-side commercial evidence is E10–E15, which is real but mostly undisclosed or not isolable to Ohtani. The club decision requires incremental discounted cash flows against an explicit alternative roster. E17 × E18 is a limited retrospective market-cost proxy: 9.4 WAR × $13.91M = $130.754M, rounded to $131M. The underlying rate prices projected-WAR cohorts. It is not actual club receipts, profit, ten-year contract value or evidence of breakeven. The decks do not use this as a headline. See [final-finance-audit.md](final-finance-audit.md) and the peer addenda.
