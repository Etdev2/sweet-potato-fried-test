## Disclaimer

Fictional parody in the form of a sports documentary. Greg, Fred, Elliott as CFO, Carolina, Markus Schultz, the former Angels executive, the colleague, the fans, the gossip clip, the memo, the buyback, the Ohio rumor, the mail room scene, the Catalina incident and Harbor Point Medical Center are invented. These characters are not real team executives or employees, and the real Dodgers ownership is not depicted. Greg is a good-natured exercise character; nothing here describes any real person's conduct. Ohtani appears only as a #17 silhouette. His contract, statistics and the commercial figures are real and cited below. Endorsement figures are worldwide estimates of Ohtani's personal income, not Dodgers revenue. The $131 million figure is an illustration pending Fred's sign-off. E10 is a secondhand estimate.
