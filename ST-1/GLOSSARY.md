# ST-1 agency valuation

This exercise compares Ohtani's value to his club and to a prospective U.S. advertiser. Each recipient has a separate economic question.

## Language

**Nominal obligation**:
The undiscounted compensation promised over the contract payment schedule.
_Avoid_: Present value, annual cash cost

**Player payment**:
Cash compensation paid to the athlete at its contractual payment date.
_Avoid_: CBT charge, funding reserve

**CBT payroll charge**:
Compensation attributed to club payroll under the collective bargaining agreement's competitive balance tax rules.
_Avoid_: Cash tax bill, signing-date DCF

**Original-decision payment DCF**:
The discounted value of the full modeled payment schedule at the stated original-decision date and analyst discount rate.
_Avoid_: Current remaining liability, official payroll value

**Funding obligation**:
The club's requirement to maintain the prescribed present value of outstanding deferred compensation.
_Avoid_: A second salary expense

**Incremental club contribution**:
Club receipts attributable to the decision after relevant costs, compared with a defined alternative roster.
_Avoid_: Athlete endorsement income, gross sponsorship revenue

**Worldwide off-field estimate**:
A third party's estimate of athlete earnings from specified non-salary activities across countries and a stated period.
_Avoid_: Disclosed U.S. endorsement fees

**U.S. activation evidence**:
A dated announcement or measurement of a campaign, product or activity directed at the U.S. market.
_Avoid_: Current rights clearance, proven sales lift

**Advertiser incremental contribution**:
Additional advertiser contribution after variable costs, measured against an appropriate campaign comparison.
_Avoid_: Recognition, gross sales

**Market-cost proxy**:
An illustrative performance comparison using a stated free-agent market methodology.
_Avoid_: Club profit, whole-contract value

**Fictional Greg**:
The invented character whose personnel events form the internal comedy story.
_Avoid_: A factual allegation about Elliott's friend
