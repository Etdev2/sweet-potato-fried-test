## Assignment

Act as a six-role sports agency. Evaluate Shohei Ohtani's contract value and U.S. endorsement appeal. Turn the evidence into three polished audience-specific decks, a financial report, and an optional fictional comedy report. Elliott is the CFO in the exercise.

## Deliverables

1. Owner and agency-leadership deck. Build the strongest evidence-based case that Ohtani is worth his contract. Explain payment timing, baseball contribution, commercial signals and uncertainties. Evaluate the 'best deal of all time' proposition against an explicit comparison, rather than inventing proof.
2. U.S. advertiser marketing deck. Explain why a brand should consider partnering with Ohtani. Include audience/category fit, original campaign concepts, rights dependencies and measurable outcomes. Carolina leads the creative strategy.
3. Personal internal Greg-versus-Fred deck. Challenge the salary argument with correct financial distinctions and sharp comedy. Introduce Fred as the new controller. Greg's firing is a fictional agency scene, not a factual HR allegation or a real employment recommendation.
4. Financial report. State sources, assumptions, calculations, sensitivities and unresolved data. Distinguish player income, club cash flow, advertiser economics and agency fees.
5. Extra credit. Write a clearly fictional comedy report in which Greg discovers his firing, makes a scene and returns the next day drunk and vomiting in the mailroom. This is invented fiction about an exercise character, not a claim about Elliott's real friend.

Decks should be available in editable PowerPoint and readable PDF formats, with source notes. A companion written report supports the finance case.

## Evidence standards

- Use current, attributable sources and give each fact its measurement period.
- Separate U.S. endorsement evidence from global earnings estimates.
- Label reported figures, estimates, illustrative scenarios and unavailable data.
- Keep salary payments, funding obligations, CBT accounting and economic present value distinct.
- Avoid double-counting player, club and sponsor value.
- Test contract-worth and advertiser-worth separately; neither is automatic proof of the other.
- Cite consequential numbers on the slide or its notes. Let the evidence determine the conclusion.
